Head Coach Hockey Privacy Policy

Smoothbrain Studios. Effective November 6, 2026.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Head Coach Hockey on Android. In "we" and "us" below, that means Smoothbrain Studios, the game's developer.

The short version

  • The game doesn't ask for your name, email or an account, and we don't run servers that receive your data.
  • Your saves stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google, and Google collects data to provide them.
  • If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.

Information stored on your phone

Your seasons, lines, settings and Pro status are saved on your phone. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them, although Android's own device backup may keep a copy in your Google account if you have backup turned on, and Google Play remembers that you bought Pro.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show full-screen ads between periods. To provide, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and the approximate location it suggests
  • device and app information, such as your phone model, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

We don't receive information from Google that identifies you personally. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

Head Coach Hockey Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the game whether your account owns Pro, so it can come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. We may receive purchase records from Google, such as an order number, date, price and country, for accounting and to handle refunds. The Google Privacy Policy applies to your payment information.

The game engine (Unity)

The game is built with Unity. Unity may collect basic technical information about your device, such as its model, operating system and graphics hardware, to understand the devices its software runs on. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before ads load. Where it applies, you can change your choice later with the PRIVACY button in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can delete or reset it in your phone's settings, under Privacy, then Ads. Menu names can vary by phone.
  • No ads: Pro removes ads from the game.
  • Remove your data from the phone: uninstall the game.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA, the UK and US states such as California, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data or targeted advertising. You won't be treated differently for using these rights, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We don't sell personal information for money. Ad data collected by Google may count as "sharing" or as a "sale" under some state laws, and the choices above and Google's own controls apply to it. Because we don't hold data that identifies you, requests about ad data are usually best sent to Google directly. You can also contact us and we'll help where we can.

Where European law applies, ad data is processed on the basis of your consent where consent is required, and otherwise on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding and protecting the game. Purchase records are processed to fulfill your purchase and meet legal obligations. Google and Unity may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

How long information is kept

Information on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. Google and Unity keep the data they collect according to their own policies. We keep any purchase records we receive for as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Security

We don't operate servers that hold your information. Data sent to Google and Unity is handled under their security practices. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure.

Children

Head Coach Hockey is intended for players 13 and older and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
